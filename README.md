# Hand-Crank-Generator
A hand crank generator with swappable gear-ratios and capable of 25V+ and low internal resistance

The generator has 10 coils arranged in a circle and includes permanent magnets which rotate on either side of the coils. When the magnets go past the coils, an alternating current voltage is induced. Each coil is rectified separately using a full bridge rectifier before the outputs are joined together, thus enabling the generated power to be converted into a usable direct current supply.

## How it works

1. The crank is manually turned.
2. The gears cause the generator to rotate faster.
3. The magnets are situated on both sides of the 10 coils and rotate around them.
4. The varying magnetic field causes an alternating current voltage to be produced in each coil.
5. Each coil is separately rectified.
The outputs are combined and made smoother.
7. The output voltage is regulated by a buck converter.
8. A fuse provides protection for the output.
9. The output voltage is displayed by means of a voltage indicator.

## Generator

The generator contains:

* 10 individual coils
* Permanent magnets on both sides of the coils
* Low-resistance windings
* Gearing between the crank and generator
* Replaceable gear ratios

The coils are designed with a low internal resistance in order to minimise losses and enable a greater current to be produced.

## Modular gearing

The gears are intended to be slid onto the shafts so that they can be taken off and replaced without the necessity of rebuilding the generator.

There are over 25 different gear arrangements available, enabling the gear ratio to be altered according to the required output.

If the gear ratio is higher, the generator will turn faster for the same crank speed, whereas a lower ratio makes the generator easier to turn.

## Crank

The mechanical advantage and the quantity of torque needed to turn the generator can be altered by adjusting the crank length.

The generator can therefore be set up for a variety of applications, ranging from low-power generation that is easier to carry out to higher-power operation.

## Electrical system

The generator offers a number of modes that can be selected and controls.

### Rectified / raw AC

A switch allows the output to be changed between:

– The DC is rectified, then combined and smoothed prior to it reaching the regulator.
– Raw AC enables the generated AC signal to be accessed directly for the purpose of testing and measurements.

### Voltage display

The voltmeter which is built into the device can be turned on or off.

It enables the display to be turned off when it is not required and cuts down on unnecessary power usage.

### Pull-up resistor

A switch is used to enable or disable the internal pull-up resistor that is employed by the control circuit.

## CAD

The mechanical design of the generator found in the CAD files includes the coil assembly, the arrangement of the magnets, the gearing and the adjustable crank.

The gears are intended to be interchangeable in order that different gear ratios may be tested without the necessity of redesigning the main generator.
It is entirely 3D printable apart from the alluminium rods and bearings, meaning it very cost effective

## Purpose

The aim of the project was to examine the relationship between mechanical input, generator speed, induced voltage and electrical power output whilst at the same time producing a practical hand-operated generator. It is able to charge a phone effortlessly, light up light bulbs, or spin motors. You can also connect the AC output to a speaker to listen to the sine wave.

The system brings together mechanical design, electromagnetic induction, electronics, power conversion and 3D printing.
